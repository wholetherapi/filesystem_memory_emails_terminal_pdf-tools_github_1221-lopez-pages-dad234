## Open Datasets

- **Metro Housing Indicators (2023)** — harmonized annual indicators for 14 municipal housing agencies; CC-BY 4.0; 1.2 GB.
- **Civic Survey Response Benchmarks (2020–2024)** — response rates and mode comparisons across 38 local surveys; CC0.
- **WeatherStation Quality Flags** — quality-control labels for 220 automatic weather stations; MIT license.

Each release includes a data dictionary, provenance notes and a reproducible processing script.
