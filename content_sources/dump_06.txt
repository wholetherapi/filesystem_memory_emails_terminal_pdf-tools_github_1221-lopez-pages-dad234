## Methods Memo: Weighting Without a Perfect Frame

This memo documents the approach used on recent household panel projects when the sampling frame is incomplete. It covers frame comparison, coverage adjustment, and sensitivity checks.

Key recommendations:
1. Reconcile frame units against administrative counts before weighting.
2. Report coverage-adjusted estimates alongside unadjusted ones.
3. Keep the adjustment code and intermediate artifacts under version control.
